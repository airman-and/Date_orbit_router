## Design Direction
- Anchor: Custom premium retail wayfinding kiosk.
- Color palette: charcoal black, ivory white, muted sand, sky blue accent.
- Typography: existing Outfit + Noto Sans KR, with tighter Korean line wrapping.
- Spacing: 8px grid, dense enough for kiosk scanning.
- Radius: 10-18px, no pill overload except route chips.
- Motion: restrained hover lift and route highlight, no decorative overload.
- Content: direct Korean labels, no ad copy, no fabricated stats.
